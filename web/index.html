The route

  1. The Forsaken Cell. A flat stone hall ending in a pair of great sealed doors.
  2. The Hollow Graveyard. A sunken grave pit, staggered tombs, and something hidden in the overgrowth.
  3. The Bellkeeper's Chapel. The ember shrine, and a portcullis that won't open from this side.
  4. The Bell Tower. Ladders, chains, a swinging scaffold, and a long way down.
  5. The Broken Ramparts. Shield knights, a broken bridge, and the lever below.
  6. The Ashen Gate. The Ashen King, beneath a burning eclipse.
